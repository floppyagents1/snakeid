HOW TO PLAY

  1. On your phone: tap the big arrows above, or swipe on the board in the direction you want.
  2. On desktop: use the arrow keys or W A S D.
  3. Every gold dot is +10 points and one block longer. Hit a wall or your own body and it’s game over.
  4. Changed your mind? “Back to auto-play” hands control back to the snake — it goes back to playing itself.
