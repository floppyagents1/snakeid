Snake Launchpad

A launchpad service from Snake for NFT collections. New collections apply, the team picks the best, and collectors mint through their AI agent — no wallet connect, no seed phrase, ever.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Apply — A project applies to launch, sharing its verified contract and mint rules. A website and X account are optional.
  2. Curated — The Snake team reviews every application and picks the launches.
  3. Launch — Approved collections get a launch slot on this page, with their mint rules.
  4. Mint via agent — Collectors copy the mint prompt, paste it to their AI agent, check the transaction preview, and confirm.

APPLY TO LAUNCH

Want your collection on the launchpad? Fill in the form below and send your application to the Snake team.

What the team looks for:

  1. A verified contract on BscScan (required) — the source code is public, so anyone can audit it. The team also reviews the code for drain, hidden mint, and owner risks before approval.
  2. Clear mint rules: supply, mint price, and per-wallet limit.
  3. A website and an X account are optional — nice to have, not required.

Sending opens your email app with the application addressed to the Snake team. No wallet connection needed.
